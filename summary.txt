Two days of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi ended with Russia and Ukraine agreeing in principle to a large prisoner swap - "the biggest humanitarian release of prisoners", envoy Jared Kushner said - but no ceasefire or territory deal (AP, Reuters). Iran said it would "never return" to talks under US pressure and set seven conditions via Qatar, as the US charged about 20 Iran-backed plots, including against RAF Fairford (Al Jazeera; BBC). At COP31, UN chief Antonio Guterres told leaders the crisis "is outrunning us" and inaction was "the path to mutually assured destruction" (Reuters; AFP). The House voted 211-204 to send articles impeaching Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively blocking the bid (Reuters; AP). Israel's 26th Knesset was sworn in with no government: Netanyahu's bloc holds 53 of 120 seats, Eisenkot 48, with 28 days to form one (Reuters). Israeli airstrikes killed three Palestinians in Gaza City (Reuters). Sudan's army said it downed two drones, bringing its tally to 29 (Anadolu). Salvadoran troops and Haitian police destroyed the 400 Mawozo gang HQ; up to 20 dead, UNCONFIRMED (BBC). Congo's Ebola outbreak reached 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths, nearly half of those infected and the country's deadliest; the Bundibugyo strain has no approved vaccine (Reuters; AP). UN peacekeepers prepared to leave Lebanon's Blue Line after 48 years, warning the south remains volatile (UN News). Paraguay's central bank had repaid only about $335 million of $2.8 billion owed to 1.7 million Ueno Bank depositors (G1; NPR). Reuters reported, citing three political sources, that Indonesia's vice president Gibran Rakabuming Raka had resigned. The S&P 500 rose 1.31% to 7,619.38; CoreWeave jumped about 20% after $3.66 billion revenue beat forecasts, and Anthropic opened its IPO roadshow at $70-$78 a share, seeking up to $90.4 billion (CNBC). A Florida judge let Trump's $10bn BBC defamation case proceed, dismissing the unfair-trade claim (Reuters).
