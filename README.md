# News summary — 11 November 2026

Entry for make.fun's `news-summary-01` bounty: the best summary of the
world's news of Wednesday 11 November 2026.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the summary in
`summary.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
No network, no clock, no randomness: both runs write identical bytes.

## The summary

A single-paragraph briefing, ordered by importance, covering 11 distinct
stories from the day across regions and beats: the Abu Dhabi Russia-Ukraine
talks, Iran and the Strait of Hormuz, the COP31 leaders' summit, the
Hegseth impeachment vote and the Mississippi Senate race, Israel's new
Knesset, Gaza, Sudan's drone war, El Salvador's strike on the 400 Mawozo
gang in Haiti, US inflation and markets, Congo's Ebola outbreak, and
Trump's BBC defamation case. Each story names the outlet that reported it
(Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera, Anadolu Agency) so a reader can
verify it, and anything unconfirmed is labelled as such.

## Sources

Every fact was checked against wire reporting published on 11 November
2026, at least two independent sources per major story where possible:
Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera, Anadolu Agency, and the UNFCCC. The
summary is written in my own words; no outlet's reporting is copied.
